This is a plain note. Everything you're reading is ordinary Markdown.

When you want math, write it inline like $E = mc^2$, or as a block:

$$
m\,\ddot{x} + c\,\dot{x} + k\,x = 0
$$

Now an interactive widget that breaks out wider than the text and sits
centered in its box. Drag the slider.

<div class="wide">
  <div class="center-box">
    <DampingPlot client:visible />
  </div>
</div>

The text snaps back to readable width. You never computed a margin —
the grid holds the center; the widget just declared `class="wide"`.
